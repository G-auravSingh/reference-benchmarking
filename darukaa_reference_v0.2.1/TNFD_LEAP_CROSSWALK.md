# TNFD / LEAP Crosswalk

Honest mapping of Darukaa constructs to TNFD Annex 2 and LEAP. **TNFD alignment is a
reporting crosswalk, not the assessment skeleton** — we assess on ecological constructs
and map to TNFD at the reporting layer. TNFD is Locate–Evaluate–Assess–Prepare over
dependencies, impacts, risks & opportunities; it is not the production of a score, and we
do not overclaim it as such.

| Darukaa construct | TNFD Annex 2 dimension | LEAP element |
|---|---|---|
| C1 Landscape context & extent | Ecosystem extent (+ parts of condition) | Locate / Evaluate |
| C2 Vegetation condition | Ecosystem condition | Evaluate |
| C3 Faunal condition | Species (abundance/activity — honestly labelled; **not** population size) | Evaluate |
| C4 Pressures & human interface | Pressures; dependencies & impacts | Assess (impacts, risks) |
| Screening layers (range overlap, KBA) | — (screening only, never scored) | Locate (prioritisation) |
| Social-dependency module (planned) | Dependencies; stakeholder | Assess / Prepare |

**Not claimed:** Extinction-risk is **not** a Darukaa-scored pillar (range-overlap gave
zero site discrimination); it is reported as an IUCN/WPA status table for **confirmed**
species only. TNFD "population size" language is not used where no population size is
measured.
