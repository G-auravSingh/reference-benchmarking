# One-week plan decisions (22 Oct 2026)

Source: "Darukaa Adaptive: One-Week Plan and Claude Project Setup", 22 Oct [DARUKAA].

| ID | Decision | Status |
|---|---|---|
| D-22-1 | **Reporting scale.** The headline panel shows the Nature Positive State of Nature indicators in their native units (ha, %, class areas) [STD]. Darukaa's 0–100 attainment score sits beside it, uses non-compensatory logic, and is labelled as Darukaa's own index [DARUKAA]. The 0–10 concern convention used in the TCF and FCF reports appears only through a documented mapping, and only where a client template needs it. | OPEN |
| D-22-2 | **Managed land.** On agroforestry and corporate sites, Nature Positive condition is reported only for the natural fragments. Managed land gets a separate, labelled managed-land result outside the Nature Positive panel. Refined by D-23-5: the split is made per pixel. | OPEN |
| D-22-3 | **Plan citations corrected (23 Oct, after reading the documents in full):** <br>• "At least 5 condition classes" comes from the Initial Measurement Guidance, pp.21–22, not the Technical Output Report (TOR). <br>• TOR p.106 says "satellite data could not reliably differentiate plantation forestry"; it does not name Dynamic World. <br>• STAR and LIFE access is described on TOR p.68, not p.67. <br>• Neither document literally prohibits netting. Our "gross loss and gross gain, never net" rule is stricter, so it is labelled [DARUKAA]. It is consistent with TOR pp.48 and 134 and Framework p.13 footnote 1. | RECORDED |
